03 / Projects & Research

Academic engineering work

Selected projects from my academic and research experience.

01

Aerodynamics of Distributed Propulsion System with Propulsive Flap

Conducted CFD simulations to analyse the lift-enhancing effect of a propulsive flap integrated with the ACA SC(2)-0412 airfoil. Modelled flap geometry in CATIA and meshed the model using ANSYS Fluent for a range of angles.

The resume reports over 15% improvement in maximum lift coefficient and a delayed stall angle. The system was considered as a potential way to improve takeoff and landing performance in hybrid and electric aircraft.

CFDCATIAANSYS Fluent
02

Analysis of Multi-Slat System for Enhanced Takeoff and Landing

Modelled multi-slat arrangements for high-lift applications, created assembly positions to evaluate deployment and retraction concepts, and generated comparative configurations for further aerodynamic evaluation.

High-lift systemsCAD modelling
03

Trapped Vortex Combustor (TVC) Research Internship

At Pegasus Aerospace Systems, analysed trapped vortex combustor configurations using CFD tools such as ANSYS Fluent. Examined flow dynamics, flame-holding characteristics, fuel-air mixing, pressure loss, emissions, and comparisons with conventional combustor designs.
